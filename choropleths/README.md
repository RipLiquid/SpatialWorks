# Choropleth Mapping

These thematic maps use normalized demographic and socioeconomic variables to communicate spatial patterns with choropleth symbology.

## Maps

- Percentage of population aged 65+ by census subdivision in the Toronto CMA
- Population change from 2006 to 2016 by Canadian census division
- Median household income by census tract in the Kitchener-Waterloo-Cambridge CMA

## Skills Demonstrated

- Sequential and diverging colour schemes
- Classification of numeric attributes
- Rates and ratios versus raw counts
- Census geography
- Thematic map design and interpretation
